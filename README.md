### My Audit Process

My research is guided by ten questions:

1. What does the code actually do?
2. Where is the interesting behavior?
3. What assumptions is the developer making?
4. Who controls the execution path?
5. Can an attacker actually trigger this?
6. What conditions need to hold for the trigger to work?
7. Can those conditions be forced?
8. What dangerous state becomes reachable?
9. Does it actually matter in a real deployment?
10. Did I miss a protection or trust assumption?
